Frequently Asked Questions

How do I edit or delete my profile data?

AI Zodiac uses decentralized local storage on your Android device (private SharedPreferences). We do not operate cloud user accounts or password databases. You can update or delete your birth parameters directly inside the app's Profile screen at any time, or remove all data via Android Settings > Apps > AI Zodiac > Storage > Clear Storage.

How do I manage advertising preferences?

Advertisements in the Android app are delivered by Google AdMob. You can manage or reset your advertising ID and ad personalization at any time through your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

Are website cookies used?

No. This website and the AI Zodiac backend APIs do not use cookies, web beacons, or third-party web tracking tools.
